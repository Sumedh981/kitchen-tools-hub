privacy notice

what this site collects

Plain-language summary — this is a student project, not a commercial service.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

With your consent, this site uses Google Analytics to understand how the tools are used, for a college data-analytics assignment. This includes page views, approximate location (city-level, from IP address), device and browser type, and interaction events (e.g. "ingredient added", "recipe saved", "budget set") listed in the project's README. No names, emails, or payment details are ever collected, because this site asks for none of those.

Analytics only runs if you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you click Decline, no analytics script loads and no data is sent to Google from your visit. You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this page, which resets the choice.

Data stored on your device

Saved recipes, your shopping list, budget entries, logged ingredient prices, and your mock subscription status are all stored only in your browser's local storage. None of this is sent to a server — there is no database behind this site. Clearing your browser data for this site removes it permanently.

Mock subscription

The "Premium" plan on this site is simulated for the assignment's analytics purposes. No payment is ever requested or processed, no card details are collected, and no real money changes hands.
